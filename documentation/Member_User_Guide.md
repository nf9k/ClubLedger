## 1. Welcome

The Membership Portal lets you access and update your club membership information at any time.

### 1.1 What You Can Do

* View your membership information
* Update your contact details
* Check your membership status
* Change your password
* Recover your account if you forget your password
* Receive automatic notifications when your record changes or your membership status changes

---

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 First Time Login

When you first join, an administrator will create your account and you will receive an email with a password reset link.

**To set up your account:**

1. Check your email for a message with subject "Password Reset Request"
2. Click the link in the email (valid for 24 hours)
3. Create your own password
4. Confirm your password and click **Reset Password**
5. You can now log in with your call sign and new password

**Your login credentials:**
* **Username**: Your call sign (uppercase, e.g., W9ABC)
* **Password**: The one you just created

**If you don't receive the email:**
* Check your spam/junk folder
* Contact an administrator

### 2.2 Regular Login

1. Go to the membership portal
2. Enter your call sign (uppercase)
3. Enter your password
4. Click **Login**

---

## 3. Password Recovery

### 3.1 Requesting a Password Reset

1. On the login page, click **Password Recovery**
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Click **Send Recovery Link**
4. Check your inbox — the link is valid for 24 hours

### 3.2 Creating Your New Password

1. Click the link in the reset email
2. Enter and confirm your new password
3. Click **Reset Password**

### 3.3 Troubleshooting

* Check spam/junk if you don't see the email
* Make sure you entered the correct email address
* If your email is not on file, contact an administrator to update it

## 4. Viewing and Updating Your Profile

### 4.1 Viewing Your Information

After logging in you will see your profile showing:

* Your call sign
* Name or group name
* Contact information (address, phone, email)
* Representative information (if applicable)
* Membership status and dues paid-through year

### 4.2 Updating Your Information

1. Make changes to any editable field
2. Click **Save Changes**
3. You will see a confirmation message and receive an email summarising what changed

**Fields you CAN update:**

* Name/Group Name
* Email Address
* Mailing Address, City, State, ZIP
* Telephone Number
* Primary Representative
* Representative Call Sign

**Fields only administrators can update:**

* **Call Sign** — contact an administrator if you have upgraded your licence
* **Membership Type**
* **Paid Through Year**

## 5. Changing Your Password

### 5.1 How to Change Your Password

1. Log in to the portal
2. Click your call sign in the top-right dropdown
3. Select **Change Password**
4. Enter your current password, then your new password twice
5. Click **Update Password**

### 5.2 Password Tips

* At least 8 characters
* Mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters
* Not easily guessable
* Different from passwords used on other sites

## 6. Understanding Your Membership Status

### 6.1 Membership Types

**FULL** — Regular voting member

**ASSOCIATE** — Non-voting member

**LIFE** — Lifetime member, no annual dues

**HONORARY** — Honorary membership

### 6.2 Paid Through Year

The **Paid Through** year shows the last year your dues are active:

* **2027**: Active — good through December 31, 2027
* **2026** (current year): Expiring — you'll need to renew for next year
* **2025** (past year): Expired — please renew

### 6.3 Status Badges

**GREEN (Active)** — Paid through a future year

**YELLOW (Expiring Soon)** — Paid through the current year; time to renew

**RED (Expired)** — Paid through a past year

### 6.4 Renewing Your Membership

1. Contact the club about renewal and payment
2. Submit your payment
3. An administrator updates your **Paid Through** year
4. You will receive an email confirming the change

## 7. Notifications

The portal sends you automatic emails in two situations.

### 7.1 Record Change Notifications

Whenever your membership record is saved — by you or by an administrator — you receive an email listing exactly what changed, for example:

```
  Paid Through: 2025  →  2027
  Member Type: ASSOCIATE  →  FULL
```

If nothing changed, no email is sent.

### 7.2 Expiration Status Notifications

The system monitors your membership status and sends an email when it changes:

* **Membership expiring soon**: you're paid through the current year and the new year has begun
* **Membership expired**: your paid-through year is now in the past
* **Membership active**: your dues have been updated and your membership is current again

You receive one notification per status change — not repeated reminders for the same status.

### 7.3 Keeping Your Email Current

To ensure you receive notifications:

* Keep your email address up to date in your profile
* Add the club's sender address to your contacts to avoid spam filtering
* Check your email regularly

### 7.4 If You Don't Receive a Notification

* Check your spam/junk folder
* Verify your email address is correct in your profile
* Contact an administrator if your status changed but you received nothing

## 8. Logging Out

Click **Logout** in the top-right dropdown when you are finished.

Your session automatically expires after 24 hours of inactivity.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I upgraded my licence and have a new call sign. How do I update it?**

Only administrators can change call signs. Contact an administrator with your old and new call signs. Once updated, log in with the new call sign.

---

**Q: Can I change my membership type?**

Membership types are set by administrators. Contact the club if you believe yours needs to change.

---

**Q: I moved. How do I update my address?**

Log in, update your address fields, and click **Save Changes**.

---

**Q: I never received a welcome email.**

Check your spam/junk folder first. If you still don't see it, contact an administrator — they can resend the password reset link.

---

**Q: Why can't I log in?**

Common reasons:
* Call sign must be entered in UPPERCASE
* Verify you're using the correct password
* If you recently changed your call sign, use the new one
* Try Password Recovery
* Contact an administrator if problems persist

---

**Q: Is my information secure?**

Yes. Passwords are encrypted with bcrypt and are never visible to administrators. Access to the full member database is restricted to administrators only.

---

**Q: Will I be notified before my membership expires?**

Yes. When the new year begins and your paid-through year falls behind, your status changes and you receive an email. This gives you a clear prompt to renew.

---

**Q: I received a record change email but didn't change anything.**

An administrator may have updated your record. The email lists exactly what changed — review it and contact an administrator if something looks incorrect.
